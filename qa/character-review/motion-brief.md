# Rift motion study

Silent interactive 600×760 character study. One existing version-six typographic Rift, original generated art and materials. Use Anidoodle character consistency principles: one authored model, pure poses, host-owned time, material-local texture. Existing limb binding and paper-grid rendering, plus fixed-length two-bone solves. Demonstrate continuous torso breathing, four planted walking steps, weighted jump and asymmetric greeting. No global rollout; reduced motion stays still.
