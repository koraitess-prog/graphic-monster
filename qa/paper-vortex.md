# Paper vortex / kinetic input / first contact

Refines the existing Anidoodle-derived printed-paper rendering. No replacement
monster anatomy or changes to saved DNA/scene snapshots.

- 38–141 visible scraps depending on answers, baked once from actual monster
  paint and clipped with twelve irregular torn contours. Whole eyes, mouths,
  hands, arms, feet and legs are interleaved with shreds.
- Three orbital currents with depth order, independent phase, flutter, paper
  folding and different velocities. Ordered selections use disciplined rings.
- The actual selection objects remain in the cloud. Assembly now pulls loose
  scraps inward for 3.2 seconds while anatomy settles, eyes last.
- Accepted drops sample recent pointer velocity. Held/tapped objects are gentle;
  fast throws add bounded, smoothly introduced momentum that naturally decays.
- After landing, the existing family motion performs a first look toward the
  visitor. A bounded gaze presenter follows the pointer without modifying scenes.

render of actual formation code: 141 sprites including 14 anatomical pieces,
three sampled frames about 11 ms worst on this host; this is not a browser/mobile
performance claim. Source scene unchanged after rendering. Build passed;
Impeccable detector returned no findings.
