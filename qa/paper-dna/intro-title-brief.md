# Intro lettering brief

Still site art, silent. Reuse Anidoodle seeded contours and variable-radius limbs, the project’s torn paper contours and engraving plates. Draw at native device resolution. Two orderly readable lines, MONSTER heavier, against #101313. Whole cut glyphs alternate with layered fragment glyphs; support and name field stay below. Intent: typography that belongs to the same printed-paper anatomy as the creatures.
